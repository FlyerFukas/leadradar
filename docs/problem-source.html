THE COMMERCIAL PROBLEM

Finding local businesses that need a website
is manual, slow, and mostly guesswork.

⚠  How it's done today

  • Open Maps, scroll listings one by one, click into each business
  • Visit every website by hand to see whether it even works
  • No way to separate no website at all from a site that quietly loses customers
  • No memory: you call the same business you already contacted last month
  • Hours of work for a handful of usable prospects

    • ✓  What LeadRadar does instead

  • One scan across a whole city, or specific districts, in minutes
  • Every website tested: HTTPS, mobile viewport, contact path, booking links, page errors
  • Each prospect scored only on verifiable evidence, never on opinion
  • Remembers everyone already reported, so a prospect never repeats
  • Output is a PDF with contact details and an outreach angle per business
